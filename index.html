The Thief

They call him Gumshoe — a shadow who slips through moonlit hallways, collecting smiles like pressed flowers. No safe is secure. No glass of water by the bedside goes unchecked. He leaves only a single mint on each pillow... and the faint scent of adhesive.

Shady Pines Retirement Residence — where corridors stretch like taffy and clocks melt over banisters. The night shift nurse swears she saw a denture waddle past Room 103 on tiny legs. The residents? They just smile. Gummy, toothless, knowing smiles. The dentures always return. Eventually. Usually cleaner. Sometimes... upgraded.

The Loot

Behold the Trophy Wall: 847 sets and counting. Partials whisper to full plates. Gold crowns glint like pirate doubloons. A single diamond-encrusted molar — Mrs. Higgins' anniversary gift, "borrowed" 1998. The crown jewel: a set of solid ivory chompers once belonging to a circus lion tamer. They still taste of sawdust and courage.

No dentures were harmed in the making of this website. Mostly.
